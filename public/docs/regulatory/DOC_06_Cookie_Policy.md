# Cookie Policy

**Version 3.1**  
**Effective: March 2026**

This Cookie Policy explains how TrashDrop uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on https://trashdrops.com. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy (DOC 04).

---

## 6.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website, serving functions such as keeping you logged in, remembering preferences, and providing anonymised usage analytics. Similar technologies include web beacons, pixels, and local storage.

---

## 6.2 Cookies We Use

| Category | Purpose | Consent Required? | Retention |
|----------|---------|-------------------|-----------|
| Strictly Necessary | Authentication, security tokens, load balancing. Without these, the Platform cannot function. | No (essential) | Session / 24 hours |
| Functional | Remember your language, region, and display preferences. | Yes | 90 days |
| Analytics | Anonymised usage statistics to improve the Platform (Google Analytics in anonymised mode). | Yes | 13 months |
| Performance | Monitor Platform performance and identify technical errors. | Yes | 30 days |

### Our Commitment

TrashDrop does not use advertising cookies, behavioural profiling cookies, or third-party tracking cookies that share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 6.3 Managing Cookies

You may withdraw or adjust consent to non-essential cookies at any time by:

- Using the Cookie Preferences tool in the website footer
- Adjusting settings in your browser
- Contacting privacy@trashdrops.com
