# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 3.1**  
**Effective: March 2026**  
**Applies to all users & officers**

---

## 7.1 Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out the standards of conduct required of all users of the TrashDrop Platform, including registered individual users, authorised municipal officers, and TrashDrop administrative staff. The AUP is incorporated by reference into the Terms of Service and the Municipal Deployment & Data Sharing Agreement.

---

## 7.2 Principles of Use

- **Accuracy:** Report only incidents you have personally observed or have reasonable grounds to believe have occurred
- **Proportionality:** Use the Platform exclusively for its intended environmental monitoring purpose
- **Respect:** Do not use the Platform in a manner that could harm, harass, or unfairly target any individual or community
- **Integrity:** Do not manipulate, falsify, or corrupt any report or Platform data
- **Confidentiality:** Do not share access credentials or disclose restricted report information to unauthorised persons

---

## 7.3 Prohibited Conduct

The following may result in immediate account suspension, permanent termination, and referral to law enforcement or regulatory authorities.

### False Reporting
- Submitting fabricated, staged, or deliberately misleading environmental incident reports
- Manipulating photographic evidence or GPS data prior to submission
- Using the Platform to file vexatious complaints against identified individuals

### Unauthorised Access and Technical Abuse
- Attempting to access data or system features beyond your authorised role
- Reverse-engineering, scraping, or extracting data without written authorisation
- Introducing malware or code designed to interfere with Platform operations
- Circumventing authentication mechanisms or security controls

### Privacy Violations
- Sharing another user's personal data obtained through the Platform with any unauthorised party
- Using the Platform to surveil, stalk, or monitor specific individuals
- Publishing personal data from reports in violation of applicable data protection law

---

## 7.4 Reporting Violations

Suspected violations should be reported to integrity@trashdrops.com. Reports may be submitted anonymously and will be treated confidentially.
