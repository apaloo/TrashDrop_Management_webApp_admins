# Privacy Policy

**Version 3.1**  
**Effective: March 2026**  
**DPO: privacy@trashdrops.com**

---

TrashDrop ("we," "our," "us") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana. We are registered as a data controller with the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

---

## 4.1 Data We Collect

### Data you provide directly
- **Account registration details:** full name, email address, phone number, and password (stored in hashed form)
- **Environmental incident reports:** descriptions, photographs, and video you submit
- **Communications you send to our support team**

### Data we collect automatically
- **Precise geolocation** at the time of report submission (with your permission)
- **Device information:** device model, operating system version, and app version
- **Usage data:** pages viewed, features used, timestamps of activity
- **Log data:** IP address, browser type, and access times

---

## 4.2 How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Data Used | Legal Basis |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Provide and operate the Platform | Account details, usage data | Performance of contract |
| Process and forward environmental reports | Location, media, incident description | Public interest / Legitimate interest |
| Send report status notifications | Email / phone number | Consent |
| Improve platform functionality | Anonymised usage data | Legitimate interest |
| Comply with legal obligations | As required by law | Legal obligation |
| Detect fraud and ensure security | Log data, access records | Legitimate interest |

---

## 4.3 Sharing Your Data

- **Municipal Authorities:** Environmental incident reports (including location data) are shared with the relevant municipal authority for the area in which the incident occurred
- **Technology service providers:** We engage third-party providers for cloud hosting, push notifications, and analytics. All providers are bound by data processing agreements
- **Legal requirements:** We may disclose data where required by law, court order, or to protect rights, property, or safety
- **Aggregated analytics:** We may share anonymised, aggregated statistics with research institutions and development partners. No individual is identifiable from such data

**We do not sell your personal data to any third party.**

---

## 4.4 Your Rights

Under Act 843 you have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion (subject to retention obligations)
- Object to processing
- Receive a portable copy of your data

Contact privacy@trashdrops.com — we will respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission of Ghana at www.dataprotection.org.gh.

---

## 4.5 Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active and for a further two (2) years after account closure, except where longer retention is required by law. Environmental evidence is retained per the schedule in DOC 01 Section 1.7.

---

## 4.6 Changes

We will notify registered users of material changes via email or in-app notification at least fourteen (14) days before changes take effect. Continued use after that date constitutes acceptance.
