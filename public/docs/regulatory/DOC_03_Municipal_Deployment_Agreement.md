## Parties

This Municipal Deployment and Data Sharing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

- **TrashDrop Limited**, operating the TrashDrop civic environmental platform at https://trashdrops.com ("Platform Operator"); and
- **The [Municipal/Metropolitan/District Assembly] of [Name]**, a public authority established under the Local Governance Act, 2016 (Act 936) of Ghana ("Municipal Authority").

---

## 3.1 Purpose

This Agreement governs the terms under which TrashDrop deploys and makes available its environmental monitoring platform to the Municipal Authority, and the conditions under which data generated through the Platform within the municipality is accessed, processed, shared, and retained.

---

## 3.2 Definitions

- **Platform Data**: All data generated through use of the TrashDrop platform within the Municipal Authority's jurisdiction, including incident reports, geolocation records, media files, audit logs, and analytics outputs.
- **Personal Data**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under Act 843.
- **Incident Report**: A georeferenced environmental report submitted by a registered user documenting a suspected environmental violation or waste management incident.
- **Anonymised Data**: Data from which personal identifiers have been irreversibly removed such that re-identification is not reasonably possible.
- **Authorised Officer**: A named employee of the Municipal Authority granted access credentials to the municipal dashboard by the Authority's designated system administrator.

---

## 3.3 Roles and Responsibilities

### Municipal Authority Undertakes To
- Designate a named Platform Coordinator responsible for liaison with TrashDrop and day-to-day deployment oversight
- Manage Authorised Officer access credentials, ensuring prompt deactivation upon change of employment or role
- Use Platform Data exclusively for environmental enforcement, waste management, and public health operations within its jurisdiction
- Ensure Authorised Officers complete the TrashDrop data protection training module prior to dashboard access
- Report any actual or suspected personal data breach to TrashDrop's DPO within 24 hours of becoming aware

### TrashDrop Undertakes To
- Provide and maintain the technology infrastructure for Platform operation within the municipality
- Implement and maintain the technical and organisational security measures described in this Agreement and the Security Policy (DOC 08)
- Process personal data only in accordance with documented instructions of the Municipal Authority and applicable law
- Provide the Municipal Authority with timely access to environmental analytics dashboards and reports
- Notify the Municipal Authority of any security incident affecting Platform Data within 48 hours of discovery

---

## 3.4 Data Ownership

- **Municipal Data Ownership.** All environmental incident data generated within the jurisdiction of the Municipal Authority belongs to and remains the intellectual property of the respective Municipal Authority. TrashDrop acts as data processor in respect of such data.
- **Platform IP.** All intellectual property in the TrashDrop platform remains the sole property of TrashDrop.
- **Anonymised Analytics.** TrashDrop retains a perpetual, non-exclusive right to use irreversibly anonymised and aggregated data for system improvement and environmental analytics development, provided no individual, jurisdiction, or specific incident can be identified.

---

## 3.5 Data Access and Processing Controls

- Authorised Officers may access incident reports, geolocation analytics, and environmental dashboards relevant to their jurisdiction
- Access is restricted through role-based permissions configured by the Municipal Authority's system administrator
- No third party shall be granted access without prior written consent of the Municipal Authority, except where required by law
- All data processing is conducted within data centres in Ghana or such other jurisdiction(s) as explicitly agreed in Schedule 1

---

|------|--------|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Initial notification to Municipal Authority | Within 48 hours of discovery | |

| 3 | Containment and remediation | Immediate upon assessment | |
| 4 | Data Protection Commission notification (where required) | Within 72 hours of breach discovery | TrashDrop (as controller) |
| 5 | Affected data subject notification | Without undue delay | Both Parties |
| 6 | Post-incident root cause analysis | Within 30 days | TrashDrop, shared with Authority |

---

## 3.7 Term and Termination

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of one (1) year, automatically renewing for successive one-year periods unless terminated. Either Party may terminate upon sixty (60) days' written notice.

**Consequences of termination:** All access credentials will be revoked within 48 hours; a complete data export will be provided within 30 days; personal data will be deleted within 90 days except where retention is required by law; confidentiality and data protection obligations survive termination.

---

## 3.8 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana. Disputes shall first be subject to good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within 30 days, the dispute shall be referred to mediation under the auspices of the Ghana Arbitration Centre, and thereafter to the courts of Ghana.
