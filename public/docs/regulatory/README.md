# TrashDrop Regulatory Policy & Legal Documentation Suite

This directory contains the complete regulatory framework and legal documentation for the TrashDrop environmental monitoring platform.

## Document Structure

### Core Regulatory Documents
- **DOC 01** - Environmental Evidence Admissibility Policy
- **DOC 02** - Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)
- **DOC 03** - Municipal Deployment & Data Sharing Agreement (Template)

### User-Facing Policies
- **DOC 04** - Privacy Policy
- **DOC 05** - Terms of Service
- **DOC 06** - Cookie Policy
- **DOC 07** - Acceptable Use Policy

### Technical & Security Framework
- **DOC 08** - Information Security Policy

## Legal Framework

These documents are drafted in conformity with Ghanaian legislation including:
- Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1994 (Act 490)
- Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)
- Public Health Act, 2012 (Act 851)
- Local Governance Act, 2016 (Act 936)
- Evidence Act, 1975 (NRCD 323)

## Regional Compliance

When operating in other ECOWAS member states, provisions of applicable national legislation are incorporated, including:
- ECOWAS Supplementary Act A/SA.1/01/10 on Personal Data Protection
- African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection (Malabo Convention, 2014)

## Document Versions

All documents are maintained at Version 3.1, effective March 2026, with annual review cycles scheduled for March 2027.

## Implementation Notes

- Each municipal deployment requires a separately executed Agreement (DOC 03)
- DPIA (DOC 02) is conducted annually or when significant processing changes occur
- Security Policy (DOC 08) aligns with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification requirements
- User-facing policies should be published on the platform website and made available in-app
